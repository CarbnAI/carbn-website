# Carbn Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 · October 2026

## 1. Brand Foundation & Voice

### 1.1 Brand personality framework and mission statement

**Mission statement**
Carbn gives every organisation a complete AI capability on the hardware it already owns, so its data never has to leave the building and its team never has to assemble AI from parts.

**Tagline**
Your entire AI stack. Your own hardware.

**Positioning statement**
For platform and infrastructure engineering leads at organisations whose data cannot leave the building, who need a real AI capability and could build one themselves, Carbn is a complete AI system that runs on the servers they already own. It delivers what a hosted AI vendor delivers (chat, workflows and integrations) with nothing leaving the network and no per-token cost. Inference clouds rent out someone else's hardware; open-source runtimes hand over parts. Carbn is the whole machine: engine, modules and platform, built to fit the hardware already in the rack.

**Audience**

| Role                                | What they need from us                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform engineering lead (primary) | Proof it was built by people like them, and that they won't be the only person who can run it |
| CISO / security                     | Evidence of rigour: isolation, audit, update path                                             |
| CIO / CTO                           | Confidence that Carbn is a durable, defensible choice                                         |
| Open-source community               | A real project, not a sales funnel                                                            |

**Character**
The engineer who has done this ten times: experienced, specific and calm. Authority comes from knowing what breaks, never from volume.

**Personality traits**

| We are        | We are not   |
| ------------- | ------------ |
| Precise       | Fussy        |
| Substantial   | Slow         |
| Serious       | Solemn       |
| Assertive     | Aggrieved    |
| Engineered    | Clinical     |
| Open          | Amateur      |
| Institutional | Bureaucratic |

**How the brand should feel**

- **Core feeling:** relief, in the sense of "this is less work than I feared".
- **Journey:** from exposed to equipped.
- **Never awe.** Carbn is something you own, not a power you borrow.

### 1.2 Tone of voice

|         | Rule                                       | Write                                                    | Not                                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| ✔ Do   | Use active voice and plain words           | "Carbn runs open models on the servers you already own." | "AI capabilities are delivered via on-premise deployment." |
| ✔ Do   | Be specific; use facts we can stand behind | "Runs on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple silicon and ARM."     | "Blazing-fast, best-in-class performance."                 |
| ✔ Do   | Say what we don't do                       | "Carbn runs inference. It does not train models."        | Implying every use case is covered                         |
| ✘ Don't | Use corporate jargon or hype               | "Deploy AI on your own servers."                         | "Unleash next-generation AI synergies."                    |
| ✘ Don't | Use passive voice or vague subjects        | "Your data stays on your network."                       | "Data sovereignty is ensured."                             |
| ✘ Don't | Attack competitors or the cloud            | "No per-token cost. You already own the hardware."       | "Stop paying the cloud tax."                               |

**Also never use:**

- emoji or exclamation marks
- "I" (write "Carbn" or "we")
- climate words: footprint, green, neutral, offset
- speed benchmarks as a headline claim
- carbon-chemistry metaphors in public copy

## 2. Visual Identity & Typography

### 2.1 Logo usage rules

**The logo**
The mark is called Slip: a C cut by one 45° fault, with the upper piece slid 4 units up and to the right along it. It comes in four forms:

- horizontal lock-up (the default)
- stacked lock-up
- mark alone
- wordmark alone

**Approved colourways**

| Colourway                  | Background | Use                                    |
| -------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Graphite                   | Light      | The default                            |
| Reversed (Graphite 50)     | Dark       | Dark backgrounds, photography, video   |
| Volt mark + white wordmark | Dark only  | The signature version                  |
| All Volt                   | Dark only  | Merchandise, signage                   |
| Mono black                 | Light      | One-colour print, engraving, embossing |
| Mono white                 | Dark       | Knock-outs, etching, video overlays    |

There is no Volt logo for light backgrounds. Volt on a light background measures 1.08:1, which makes it invisible.

**Minimum size**

| Form               | Screen     | Print     | Embroidery / signage |
| ------------------ | ---------- | --------- | -------------------- |
| Mark               | 24px tall  | 10mm      | 20mm                 |
| Horizontal lock-up | 140px wide | 60mm wide | 115mm wide           |
| Stacked lock-up    | 100px wide | 45mm wide | 85mm wide            |

Below the horizontal minimum, use the stacked lock-up.

**Clear space**

- **Unit:** clear space is **x**, the height of the mark's upper piece (about 46% of the mark's height).
- **Rule:** keep x clear on every side, measured from the visible edges of the logo. Nothing enters this space except the tagline, at the spacing used in the supplied files.
- **Example:** a 300px-wide horizontal lock-up needs about 24px of clear space.

**Restrictions**

1. **Do not distort.** No stretching, squashing, rotating or skewing, and never redraw the 45° cuts or the slide.
2. **Do not recolour.** Use only the approved colourways. Never put a Volt logo on a light background.
3. **Do not add effects.** No drop shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, bevels or transparency.

Also, never put the logo on busy imagery without a solid field behind it, and never retype the wordmark; always use the supplied files.

### 2.2 Typography & hierarchy

**Typefaces**

| Use                          | Typeface           | Settings                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Display and headings         | Archivo (variable) | Display: width 75, weight 700. Headings: width 100, weight 600 |
| Body text and interface      | Archivo (variable) | Body: width 100, weight 400. Interface: width 87, weight 500   |
| Labels                       | Archivo            | Width 125, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing +0.10em       |
| Code, data, technical labels | Azeret Mono        | Weight 400; 500 for emphasis                                   |

- **One family:** Carbn uses a single type family, with each role set at a different width rather than in a different typeface.
- **Licence:** both fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts.
- **Fallbacks:** if Archivo isn't installed in Office, use Arial Narrow Bold for headings and Arial for body.

**Formatting rules**

- **Display:** at most one display line per page or slide.
- **Case:** sentence case for headings; uppercase only for small labels.
- **Body text:** never below 16px on mobile, with lines 60–75 characters long.
- **Alignment:** left-aligned, never justified. Centre only short lines on cards and banners.
- **Emphasis:** use weight 600, not italics or underline. Underline is reserved for links.
- **Tables:** numbers use tabular (fixed-width) figures.

### 3.2 Accessibility & contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA)

We meet WCAG 2.2 level AA, the level most accessibility regulations point to:

- **Body text:** at least 4.5:1.
- **Large text** (24px+, or 19px+ bold): at least 3:1.
- **Borders, icons, focus rings and chart lines:** at least 3:1.

| Background           | Allowed for all text (≥ 4.5:1)                                                                                 | Large text, icons and borders only (3–4.5:1) | Never                                       |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Graphite 50 #F4F5F7  | Graphite 950 (18.3), Graphite 700 (9.1), Graphite 600 (6.8), Fault Red (4.5)                                   | Graphite 500 (4.4)                           | Volt (1.1), white, Graphite 400 and lighter |
| Graphite 100 #E6E8EA | Graphite 950 (16.2), Graphite 700 (8.1), Graphite 600 (6.0)                                                    | Graphite 500 (3.9), Fault Red (4.0)          | Volt, white, Graphite 400 and lighter       |
| Volt #D0FE00         | Graphite 950 (16.9)                                                                                            | (none)                                       | White and Graphite 50 (1.1), Fault Red      |
| Fault Red #E10033    | White (4.9), Graphite 50 (4.5)                                                                                 | Graphite 950 (4.0)                           | Volt                                        |
| Graphite 950 #08090B | Graphite 50 (18.3), white (19.9), Volt (16.9), Graphite 300 (8.9), Graphite 400 (6.2), Fault Red #FE003A (5.0) | Graphite 500 (4.1), Fault Red #E10033 (4.0)  | Graphite 600 and darker                     |
| Graphite 900 #181B1E | Graphite 50 (15.9), Volt (14.7), Graphite 300 (7.8), Graphite 400 (5.4)                                        | Graphite 500 (3.6), Fault Red #FE003A (4.3)  | Graphite 600 and darker                     |

**Rules**

1. **Volt is never text on a light background.** On light pages Volt is a fill with Graphite 950 text on top, and every Volt fill carries a 1.5px Graphite 950 border.
2. **No light text on Volt.** Never put white or Graphite 50 text on Volt.
3. **Graphite 500 on light falls just short of AA** (4.4:1). Use it only for large text, icons and borders; use Graphite 600 for small secondary text.
4. **Fault Red on dark:** use the dark variant, #FE003A, for text on dark backgrounds.
5. **Never use colour alone to show a state.** Always add a label or an icon.

## 4. Web Design (UI/UX) Guidelines

### 4.1 Component styling

**Everywhere**

- **Corners:** radius 0. Rounded corners are not used.
- **Borders:** 1px for dividers, 1.5px for components, 3px for section rules and focus rings.
- **Shadows:** no soft or blurred shadows.

**Buttons**

| Text           | Graphite 950                                   | Graphite 950 (light) / Graphite 50 (dark)             |

| Border radius  | 0                                              | 0                                                     |
| Padding        | 12px 20px desktop; 12px 16px mobile            | Same                                                  |

## 5. Social Media Design Templates

### 5.1 Platform adaptations

**Rules for every graphic**

- One idea per graphic.
- One Volt element per graphic: a Volt background, a highlight or the mark.
- Backgrounds are graphite or Volt.
- Headlines in Archivo display weight; numbers and labels in Azeret Mono.
- No photos of server racks, GPUs or server rooms.

**Instagram carousels (4:5, 1080 × 1350)**

- **Slide 1:** a headline of at most 8 words, with the mark in the bottom-left.
- **Middle slides:** one point per slide, a headline plus at most 30 words, and a slide counter ("02 / 07") in Azeret Mono in the top-right.
- **Backgrounds:** the same background throughout, with at most one Volt slide, used for the key point.
- **Last slide:** horizontal lock-up, tagline and call to action.

**LinkedIn text graphics (1:1 at 1200 × 1200, or 4:5 at 1080 × 1350)**

- **Content:** one statement or one number per graphic. Statements at most 12 words, set in display weight. A number in display weight gets a one-line caption in Azeret Mono.
- **Highlight one phrase:** a Volt band behind graphite text on light, or Volt text on dark.
- **Source line:** source or date in Azeret Mono at the bottom; lock-up in the bottom-left.

**Banners and covers**
Use the logo pack files for X, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub and Facebook. The lock-up and tagline are centred and kept clear of the lower-left corner, where the profile photo overlaps.

**Profile pictures**
Mark only. Graphite background with the Volt mark by default; Volt background with the graphite mark as the alternate. 1024px master file.

**Video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)**

- **Captions:** Archivo weight 600, Graphite 50 on a Graphite 950 band.
- **Opening and closing:** open on the mark's slide; close on the lock-up and tagline.

### 5.2 Aspect ratios & asset rules

| Format                        | Ratio  | Size (px)   | Use                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Square post                   | 1:1    | 1080 × 1080 | Instagram, LinkedIn and X feeds                          |
| Portrait post                 | 4:5    | 1080 × 1350 | Instagram feed, carousels, LinkedIn. Preferred for feeds |
| Story / Reel / Short / TikTok | 9:16   | 1080 × 1920 | Stories and vertical video                               |
| Link preview                  | 1.91:1 | 1200 × 630  | Website previews; LinkedIn and X link posts              |
| Landscape video               | 16:9   | 1920 × 1080 | YouTube, LinkedIn video                                  |
| YouTube thumbnail             | 16:9   | 1280 × 720  | Video covers on YouTube                                  |

**Static posts**

- Headlines at most 12 words.
- One Volt element.
- Export as PNG (sRGB) for graphics; JPG only for photographs.

**Video covers (9:16)**

- Design at 1080 × 1920, but keep the title and logo inside the central 1080 × 1350 area, because feeds and profile grids crop covers.
- Titles at most 5 words.

**YouTube thumbnails**
Titles at most 5 words. Keep the bottom-right corner clear for the video-length badge.

**Video export**
MP4, H.264, sRGB.

### 5.3 Safe zones & logo placement

| Format                              | Safe zone                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feed posts (1:1, 4:5) and carousels | 64px margin on every side. Nothing important outside it                                      |
| Stories (9:16)                      | Keep the top 250px and bottom 250px clear                                                    |
| Reels, TikTok, Shorts (9:16)        | Keep the top 250px, bottom 420px and right 120px clear (app buttons and captions)            |
| Banners                             | Platform-specific; see the logo pack README (for example, YouTube's centred 1546 × 423 area) |

Platforms change their interfaces, so re-check these safe zones each quarter.

**Logo placement**

| Format                                 | Placement                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feed posts and carousels               | Horizontal lock-up anchored bottom-left on the 64px margin, 200–240px wide on a 1080px canvas, never below 140px |
| Stories and vertical video             | Lock-up bottom-left, resting on the bottom safe line: 250px up for stories, 420px for Reels                      |
| Brand cards, banners, video end frames | Centred lock-up with the tagline below                                                                           |
| Profile pictures                       | Mark only, centred, filling 58% of the canvas                                                                    |

- One logo per graphic, in the same corner across a campaign.
- Clear space x always applies.
- Volt-mark versions go on graphite; the graphite logo goes on Volt; never Volt on light.
